Hi, I'm Ali!

I am a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at Michigan State University. I developed Optimal Inferential Control (OIC) for scalable and efficient control of deep learning dynamical systems. I obtained my M.Sc. at the University of Kansas, where I developed heavy-tailed Bayesian inferential motion planning (BIMP) for robust decision-making under uncertainty. I have also completed two research internships: at the General Motors Cruise team, working on large-scale autonomous-driving simulation and ML pipelines, and at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories, developing physics-based ML and optimization methods.

Research · Optimal Inferential Control

Scalable optimal control through Bayesian inference

We develop a mathematical framework that casts optimal control as an equivalent Bayesian inference problem, and develop GPU-parallel ensemble methods for neural-network-based and high-dimensional dynamical systems.

Autonomous vehicles · Planning

Uncertainty-aware motion planning with learned dynamics

Data and training pipelines for efficient training of a vehicle dynamics system model using real-world data on GPUs. We use the models to do Heavy-tailed Bayesian inferential motion planning (BIMP) and conventional trajectory-optimization pipelines. Heavy-tailed Student’s-t models provide robustness to non-Gaussian uncertainty and outliers.
