Hi, I'm Ali!

I am a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at Michigan State University. I developed Optimal Inferential Control (OIC) for scalable and efficient control of deep learning dynamical systems. I obtained my M.Sc. at the University of Kansas, where I developed heavy-tailed Bayesian inferential motion planning (BIMP) for robust decision-making under uncertainty.

I have also completed two internships: at the Cruise/General Motors, working on large-scale autonomous-driving simulation and machine learning (ML) pipelines, and at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL), developing physics-based ML and optimization methods.

Industry experience

  • Cruise/General Motors — AI/ML SWE Intern, 2026: Large-scale ML for autonomous-driving simulations; built data and training pipelines using distributed multi-GPUs in PyTorch.
  • Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories — Research Intern, 2024: Physics-based ML for thermofluid systems; Neural surrogate models that are physically consistent and scalable.
