General Motors · Cruise Team · 2026

Vehicle motion reconstruction

An ML system that reconstructs missing vehicle motion in recorded-driving simulations. Large-scale data pipelines processed multiple TBs of raw tracking data across 38,000+ highway scenes. Large-scale training used multiple H100 GPUs with PyTorch DDP, NCCL, and mixed precision.

Error compared with the team’s prior baseline on approximately 58,000 held-out vehicle trajectories.

Machine learning · Bayesian control

Optimal control through Bayesian inference

Optimal Inferential Control (OIC) reformulates optimal control as Bayesian inference. GPU-compatible ensemble methods solve the resulting inference problem for learned vehicle models, high-dimensional fluids, and thermofluid systems.

Tensor-variate ensemble Kalman smoothing: up to 18.6× faster computation and approximately 11× lower memory use than a vectorized GPU baseline.

Neural ODEs · Continuous-time control

Continuous-time control of Neural ODEs

Continuous-time model predictive inferential control applied to learned dynamical models, including a soft-robot manipulator. Tested configurations ran up to 200× faster than IPOPT on GPU in tested benchmarks.

MERL · Energy systems · 2024

Physics-constrained modeling of HVAC systems

A physics-constrained Neural ODE–GRU model for vapor-compression HVAC systems. With limited real-world data, it improved forecasting accuracy by 14% and achieved a 5.7× runtime speedup versus the prior modeling approach.

Autonomous vehicles · Planning

Motion planning with learned vehicle dynamics

GRU and ResNet vehicle models trained on real driving data support Bayesian planning and trajectory optimization. Student’s-t distributions, sequential Monte Carlo, and ensemble Kalman smoothing address non-Gaussian uncertainty and outliers.
